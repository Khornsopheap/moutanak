Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about becoming a successful seller on Moutanak.

Still have questions?

Our support team is here to help 24/7 in Khmer and English.

What documents do I need to register?
You need: Valid Cambodian National ID (front & back), selfie holding your ID, Tax Identification Number (TIN), business registration (if applicable), bank account details, and proof of business address.
How long does verification take?
Standard verification is 24–48 hours. Businesses with a valid CAMREGS registration and TIN may be approved within 6 hours through our express lane.
How do I get paid?
Payouts are sent weekly via ABA, ACLEDA, or Wing Money to your registered account for all orders fulfilled in the previous 7 days.
Is delivery handled by Moutanak?
Yes! We provide full logistics support, helping you deliver fresh from farm to door anywhere in the country.
